# Post-alignment fixes for 0017 (lead workflow wf_fa323a0b-69e, 2026-10-20)

Each item was found by a cross-document reviewer and confirmed by a skeptical second reviewer. Some items repeat from two boundaries; apply once. Lead decisions in the brief override the fix text where they differ.

## 1. [minor] F17 M1 step 6 says the bundle location comes from `DaemonDeps.bundle` (F15 step 4), which M1 does not list as a prerequisite

- Boundary: install-catalog
- Evidence and reasoning: Confirmed. 0017:588 (step 6) includes 'lokasi bundle dari `DaemonDeps.bundle`'. `DaemonDeps.bundle` and `StartConfig.current_exe` are built in 0015 step 4 (0015:211, :502). Neither the M1 header (0017:581) nor scope 02:57 lists F15. Step 11 (0017:602, M4 'needs fitur 15: `AppBundle`') also claims 'lokasi catalog dari `DaemonDeps.bundle` spec 0015'. The scope is consistent with M4 owning it: M1 mentions only `STACKLY_CATALOG_DIR`, and M4 has 'catalog location from `AppBundle`'. A developer resolves it by reading step 11, so this is drift inside 0017 rather than a blocked prerequisite.
- Fix: In 0017 step 6, replace 'lokasi bundle dari `DaemonDeps.bundle`' with '`CatalogState::NotConfigured` saat tanpa `STACKLY_CATALOG_DIR` (cabang `Bundled` dibangun langkah 11)'. That leaves the `DaemonDeps.bundle` derivation only in step 11.

## 2. [minor] Catalog `NotConfigured` = unavailable turns existing in-process daemon tests from `Ready` to `Degraded`; 0017 only covers SpawnedDaemon

- Boundary: install-catalog
- Evidence and reasoning: Confirmed against code. In-process tests build `DaemonConfig::default()` (apps/daemon/tests/server/common.rs:130) and never go through `main`, so they have no `STACKLY_CATALOG_DIR` and no bundle. Under 0017:314-317, 'Tanpa override, lokasi selalu dari aturan di atas', which gives `NotConfigured`. 0017:309 makes that `unavailable`, and the daemon becomes `Degraded`. lifecycle.rs:81, :339, :443, and :742 assert `DaemonHealthStatus::Ready`. 0017:318 promises 'test daemon yang sudah ada tetap melihat status `ok`' only through SpawnedDaemon's empty catalog. The F17 step 6 builder will hit failing tests and must pick a remedy the spec does not state.
- Fix: In 0017:318 and step 6, add: 'test di dalam proses (`Site` di `apps/daemon/tests/server/common.rs`) memasang `DaemonConfig.catalog = Some(CatalogOverride::Preloaded(Catalog::for_tests(&[])))` secara default, sehingga assert `Ready` yang ada tetap berlaku'.

## 3. [major] F17 M1 step 6 needs spec 0015 `AppBundle`/`DaemonDeps.bundle` without declaring it, and M4 step 11 claims the same bundle location

- Boundary: scope-prereqs
- Evidence and reasoning: Confirmed. In spec 0017, step 6 (:588) includes 'lokasi bundle dari `DaemonDeps.bundle`', and :314 forbids any second bundle derivation ('tidak ada turunan bundle kedua'). The M1 header (:581) and scope 02:57 list only the spec 0009/0004 text prerequisites. `AppBundle` is built in 0015 step 1 and `DaemonDeps.bundle` in 0015 step 4 (:499, :502, :211). Neither exists in code on main. M4 step 11 (:602) separately claims 'lokasi catalog dari `DaemonDeps.bundle` spec 0015', and scope 02:60 puts 'catalog location from `AppBundle`' under M4. A session building F17 M1, which is meant to be the first phase 02 build, reaches step 6 and needs an F15 field that it may not derive itself. It also cannot tell whether that work belongs to step 6 or step 11, so two sessions could each build it. One point in the finding is overstated: F15 steps 1 to 4 are not gated by the step 0 approval, since 0015:516 says they can run while step 0 waits. The dependency can therefore be met; it is just undeclared and double-claimed.
- Fix: Move the bundle branch wholly into M4 step 11, matching scope 02:60. In 0017 step 6 (:588), replace 'lokasi bundle dari `DaemonDeps.bundle`' with '`STACKLY_CATALOG_DIR` dan `NotConfigured` saja; cabang `Bundled` dari `DaemonDeps.bundle` di langkah 11 (`// provisional: F17 step 11`)'. Keep step 11 as the owner.
