# Post-alignment fixes for 0016 (lead workflow wf_fa323a0b-69e, 2026-10-20)

Each item was found by a cross-document reviewer and confirmed by a skeptical second reviewer. Some items repeat from two boundaries; apply once. Lead decisions in the brief override the fix text where they differ.

## 1. [major] F16 milestone 1 step 1 needs `BackgroundAgentState` from F15 step 10 but does not list that prerequisite

- Boundary: gui-shell
- Evidence and reasoning: 0016:74 and :358 (step 1) build `OnboardingFooter.primaryLabel(step:agentState: BackgroundAgentState?)`, passing nil in milestone 1. The type is a StacklyUI Swift type defined at 0015:296 and built in 0015 step 10 (0015:516, F15 M3). It is not a generated protocol DTO: the protocol additions at 0015:351 do not include it, and it does not exist on main or on the f13-wiring branch. The prerequisites for 0016 M1 (0016:354, scope 02:38) name only F13 M4 and step 16, and only milestone 2 needs F15 M3. `UserHome` has a 'create it here if F15 M3 has not landed' rule (0016:162), but `BackgroundAgentState` has none. A step 1 started before F15 M3 does not compile. A local stub would then collide with F15 step 10's definition in `StacklyUI/Agent/`.
- Fix: Add 'spec 0015 langkah 10 (`BackgroundAgentState` di `StacklyUI/Agent/`)' to the prerequisites of 0016 milestone 1 in the Build plan intro at 0016:354, and mirror it in scope 02 line 38.

## 2. [minor] No ordering between F15 M3 (steps 10 and 12) and F16 M1 steps 2 and 3, though both edit ShellRuntime, ShellBootstrap.assemble, and DaemonModel

- Boundary: gui-shell
- Evidence and reasoning: 0015:518 (step 12) installs `CommandLineToolModel` in `ShellRuntime` and `didBecomeActive()`. 0015:301 has `BackgroundAgentModel` owned by `DaemonModel`, and it needs the root override Bool. 0016:359 (step 2) changes `ShellRuntime.init`, `live`, and `assemble`, and adds `DaemonModel.rootOverrideActive` 'kalau fitur 15 belum menambahkannya'. 0016:162 (step 3) creates `UserHome` if F15 M3 has not landed. K18 SEQ 6 endorses 'whichever consumes first', and K18 SEQ 4 serialized F16 step 2 against F13 step 16 for exactly these files. No document serializes F15 M3 against F16 M1: scope 02:21 and :38 and 0016:354 are silent, and both milestones become eligible as soon as F13 step 16 merges. The result is a merge collision, not incompatible designs, because the names agree once finding 7 is fixed. That makes this minor, not major. The step 2 commit text for 0013 (0016:346), which says ShellRuntime holds `CommandLineToolModel`, is minor drift of the same kind.
- Fix: Add to the 0016 milestone 1 intro (0016:354): 'Langkah 2 dan 3 tidak berjalan bersamaan dengan milestone 3 fitur 15 (langkah 10 dan 12), karena keduanya mengubah `ShellRuntime.swift`, `ShellBootstrap.assemble`, dan `DaemonModel.swift`; yang di-merge kedua memakai `rootOverrideActive` dan `UserHome` dari yang pertama.' Mirror it in scope 02 lines 21 and 38.
