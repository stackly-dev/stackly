Unlike `versionCheck`, which 0019:213 covers explicitly, the spec has no sentence saying step 4 omits the block. A step 4 developer would get `unknown_field` from `provider check` and would then read :551.
- Fix: Change 0021 step 2 (index.md:683) to 'Blok `connection` di paket PostgreSQL dan fake (paket NATS menulisnya di langkah 2 spec 0023, commit yang sama dengan readiness)'. Make the same change in the 0009:87 'Dibangun di' cell and scope 02:136. Also add to 0019 near :213 one sentence saying the step 4 package omits the `connection` block until spec 0021 step 2 adds it.
