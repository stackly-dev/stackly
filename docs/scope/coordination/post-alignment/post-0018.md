Each item was found by a cross-document reviewer and confirmed by a skeptical second reviewer. Some items repeat from two boundaries; apply once. Lead decisions in the brief override the fix text where they differ.

## 1. [minor] 0018 commit step says install_conflict leaves hint `manual`, but the 0007 engine rule and 0018:446 give `resume`

- Boundary: install-catalog
- Evidence and reasoning: Confirmed. 0018:285 says '`runtime.install_conflict` dengan `path`, dan operasi `needs_recovery` dengan hint `manual`'. 0018:446 says 'gagal di `commit` atau `finalize` berarti `needs_recovery` dengan hint `resume` (langkah `commit` repeatable)'. The engine computes the hint itself (0007:222; crates/core/src/operation/state.rs:385-413), and work has no way to override it. `install_runtime` is resumable (0007:416) and `commit` is repeatable (0018:100), so the result is always `resume`. The :285 sentence cannot be built without a 0007 change, while :446 matches the engine. A developer reading both and the engine follows :446. That makes this an internal drift, not a cross-milestone incompatibility. The AC-13 test list (0018:639) does not assert the hint.
- Fix: In 0018:285, replace 'hint `manual` (doctor fitur 34)' with 'hint `resume` dari aturan hint spec 0007 (langkah `commit` repeatable); resume gagal lagi dengan kode yang sama sampai folder itu dibereskan (doctor fitur 34) atau operasi di abandon'.
